# Pixel Pushers

A single-page app that fills your screen with a living field of one-of-a-kind pixels that move, repel, and scramble to fill every empty space — just like busy children grabbing the last seats.

No build step, no dependencies, no install. Just open `index.html`.

## Try it

Open the deployed site: <https://simplesense.github.io/pixel-pushers/>

Or run locally: double-click `index.html` (or `python3 -m http.server`).

## What it does

- Generates 30 new pixels every second, non-stop, until the screen is full
- Every pixel is unique — its own color, size, and movement style
- Pixels never overlap and actively repel each other
- Pixels seek out and fill every empty space on screen
- Pixels are repelled by your mouse cursor and will never touch it
